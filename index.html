Privacy Policy

Qine Corner – Read. Reflect. Connect.

Qine Corner values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email address, optional profile photo)
  • Authentication data (email login or Google Sign-In)
  • Reading activity (books read, progress, goals, streaks)
  • Content interactions (articles, comments, likes, book clubs)
  • Preferences (notifications, theme, privacy settings)
  • Device and usage information (for app performance and debugging)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To personalise your reading experience
  • To track reading goals, progress, and achievements
  • To enable community features such as book clubs and discussions
  • To send important notifications (if enabled)
  • To improve app performance and user experience

3. Authentication & Third-Party Services

Qine Corner supports email authentication and Google Sign-In. When using Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) as permitted by Google.

We do not access your Google password or private Google account data.

4. Notifications

We may send notifications related to reading goals, book club updates, or important account events. You can control notification preferences at any time in the app settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is only shared when necessary to provide core app functionality (such as authentication or secure data storage) and only with trusted service providers.

6. Data Security

  • Secure storage and encryption where appropriate
  • Restricted internal access to user data
  • Regular system monitoring and updates

7. Your Rights

  • Access and review your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data where applicable
  • Control notification and privacy settings

8. Children’s Privacy

Qine Corner is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
